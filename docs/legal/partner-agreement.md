# ORBIT Partner (Videographer) Independent Contractor Agreement

**Entity:** Orbit QuickContent Technologies Private Limited ("Company")  
**Party:** Certified Videography Partner ("Partner")  
**Effective Date:** Upon digital onboarding & verification

---

## 1. Independent Contractor Relationship
1.1. The Partner acknowledges that they are an **independent contractor** and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of Orbit.  
1.2. The Partner maintains complete discretion over their working hours, acceptance of dispatch offers, equipment selection, and geographical operational zones.

---

## 2. Payouts, Commission & Taxes
2.1. Partner earnings per shoot are clearly indicated on each incoming dispatch card (e.g. ₹500 for Personalized, ₹1,500+ for UGC Pro).  
2.2. Earnings are credited to the Partner's in-app Wallet immediately upon final client delivery and verification.  
2.3. Wallet withdrawals are processed to the Partner's verified bank account or UPI handle via automated payment settlement rails (Cashfree Payouts).  
2.4. Applicable Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194C / 194O of the Income Tax Act, 1961 shall be deducted where mandated.

---

## 3. Equipment & Code of Conduct
3.1. Partners agree to utilize professional-grade capture equipment (4K/60fps capable smartphones, gimbals, or cinema rigs) and maintain audio clarity.  
3.2. Partners must uphold professional on-location etiquette and adhere to client project briefs.

---

## 4. Media Transfer & Confidentiality
4.1. Partners agree to upload all raw footage directly to the Orbit secure cloud repository immediately following shoot completion.  
4.2. Partners shall not disclose or commercially exploit client unreleased footage without written permission.
