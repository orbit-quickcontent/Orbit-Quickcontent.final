# ORBIT Partner Agreement
**Status:** DRAFT (Requires Legal Review)

This Partner Agreement establishes an independent contractor relationship between ORBIT ("Company") and the professional videographer ("Partner").

## 1. Independent Contractor Classification
The Partner operates as an independent contractor, not an employee. 
- ORBIT provides leads through its hyperlocal marketplace platform.
- Partner controls the method of performing the videography services, provided the final product meets ORBIT quality standards.

## 2. Platform Usage & Verification
Partners must complete offline training, identity verification (KYC), and Bank Account verification prior to platform activation. ORBIT issues a unique Activation Code upon successful onboarding.

## 3. Financial Terms
- **Earnings:** For each successfully completed and delivered booking, the Partner will receive the predetermined rate (e.g., ₹500 for standard packages).
- **Withdrawals:** Withdrawals are processed through Cashfree to the Partner's verified bank account upon request.
- **TDS:** ORBIT may deduct applicable taxes (TDS) as per Indian law.

## 4. Responsibilities
- Arrive at the client location on time.
- Use professional-grade equipment as verified during onboarding.
- Upload raw footage promptly after the shoot via the ORBIT App.
- Do not bypass the ORBIT platform to solicit direct work from ORBIT clients.

## 5. Content Rights
All raw footage and final media assets created for an ORBIT booking are the intellectual property of the Client and ORBIT. Partners retain no rights to use or distribute the footage.

## 6. Liability
ORBIT is not responsible for equipment damage, injury, or loss during a shoot.
