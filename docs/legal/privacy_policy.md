# ORBIT Privacy Policy
**Status:** DRAFT (DPDP Act 2023 Compliant)

ORBIT respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data.

## 1. Data Collection
We collect:
- **Clients:** Name, phone, email, payment details, booking locations.
- **Partners:** Name, phone, email, KYC (PAN/Aadhaar), Bank details, Live GPS location (when online/on-duty).
- **Editors:** Name, email, bank details.

## 2. GPS Location (Partners)
We collect continuous background GPS location from Partners *only* when they are marked as 'ONLINE' to dispatch nearby bookings accurately and provide Clients with live ETAs.

## 3. Usage of Media
Raw footage uploaded by Partners is temporarily stored securely for Editor processing and Client delivery.

## 4. Data Retention
Financial logs and KYC data are retained as required by Indian law (up to 7 years). Server logs are retained for 30-90 days.

## 5. Rights under DPDP Act
Users may request data access, correction, or deletion by contacting privacy@orbit-quickcontent.com.
