# ORBIT Platform — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026  
**Entity:** Orbit QuickContent Technologies Private Limited ("ORBIT", "we", "our", or "us")

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Orbit operates a hyperlocal video creation marketplace connecting content creators ("Clients"), videographers ("Partners"), and post-production specialists ("Editors"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, process, and protect your personal data in accordance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable data protection regulations.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect
We collect data necessary to fulfill booking requests and ensure platform integrity:
- **Identity & Contact Details:** Full name, phone number, email address, profile avatar.
- **Location & Geolocation:** Real-time GPS location (for on-demand dispatch, partner tracking, and route calculations).
- **Payment & Financial Data:** Transaction reference IDs, GST details, bank account/UPI ID for partner payouts (processed via PCI-DSS compliant gateways Razorpay & Cashfree).
- **Media & Content:** Raw video footage, project briefs, brand assets, and edited reels.
- **Technical & Device Information:** Device identifier, IP address, OS version, app telemetry.

---

## 3. Purpose of Processing (Lawful Grounds)
Your personal data is processed under explicit consent and contractual necessity:
1. Matching clients with nearby certified videographers.
2. Facilitating real-time shoot tracking and ETA estimation.
3. Enabling secure cloud upload of footage to editing teams.
4. Processing payments, calculating platform commissions, and releasing partner earnings.
5. Fraud prevention, trust & safety verification, and legal dispute resolution.

---

## 4. DPDP Act Compliance & Data Principal Rights
As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to:
- **Access & Summary:** Request a summary of personal data being processed.
- **Correction & Erasure:** Update inaccurate details or request deletion of account data.
- **Grievance Redressal:** Contact our designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) at `privacy@orbit-quickcontent.com`.
- **Nomination:** Nominate an individual to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Security
- Raw shoot footage is retained on encrypted cloud storage (S3/Cloudflare R2) for 30 days post-delivery to allow client revisions, after which it is automatically scheduled for permanent deletion.
- Financial transaction logs are retained for statutory periods required under Indian tax laws.
- All communications and data in transit are protected using TLS 1.3 encryption.
