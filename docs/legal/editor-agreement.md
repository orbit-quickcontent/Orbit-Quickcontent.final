# ORBIT Post-Production Editor Agreement

**Entity:** Orbit QuickContent Technologies Private Limited ("Company")  
**Party:** Certified Post-Production Specialist ("Editor")  
**Effective Date:** Upon editor onboarding & qualification test pass

---

## 1. Scope of Post-Production Services
The Editor provides professional editing, color grading, sound synchronization, captioning, and brand DNA styling on raw footage ingested through the Orbit Editor Web Platform.

---

## 2. Turnaround Time & Express SLA
2.1. Standard editing turnaround targets **45 to 90 minutes** from the time an editor claims a raw footage batch.  
2.2. Editors are assigned tasks on a first-claim basis through the Editor Dashboard feed.

---

## 3. Remuneration & Quality Standards
3.1. Editors are compensated per completed project delivered at or above platform QA benchmarks (e.g. ₹300 - ₹800 depending on reel complexity and brand assets).  
3.2. Revision requests from clients within the allowed package threshold must be resolved within 30 minutes of submission.

---

## 4. Brand DNA & Asset Confidentiality
4.1. Client brand assets (logos, typography, color palettes, product footage) are strictly confidential and must not be exported for personal portfolio usage without prior authorization.
